---
title: Control Center
description: Quick settings and the way into every system page.
---

The Control Center is the panel that slides in from the right. It is where the
system lives, so you do not have to hunt through separate windows to change how
your machine behaves.

## Quick settings

Open it from the cluster on the right of the panel. At a glance you get the
things you reach for most:

- Network, including Wi-Fi and VPN.
- Sound, with a volume slider and a way to pick input and output devices.
- Brightness.
- Battery status and the power profile.
- Power actions: shut down, restart, and lock.

Your avatar and user details sit at the top.

## Settings pages

From the Control Center you can step into the full settings, one page per area:
desktop and wallpaper, sound, network, displays, keyboard, accounts, apps,
bluetooth, region and language, date and time, notifications, accessibility,
plugins, and system information.

## The panel cluster

Some of this is mirrored in the panel itself. The cluster on the right shows the
current network, the volume, and the battery level without opening anything, and
the clock and notification button sit beside it.
